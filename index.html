Pilot Demonstration Script & Verification Protocol

Step 1 — Prime Audio & Voice Activation
Click the VOICE button. Browser speech synthesis is automatically primed for mobile and desktop audio. Speak any 5-digit ZIP code (e.g., 90210, 77030, or 10010).
Step 2 — Immediate Microphone Release & Audio Dispatch
Upon receiving the 5-digit ZIP, the microphone session terminates immediately. The application matches the facility, centers the map view, applies the VISN color scheme, and vocalizes the facility name and direct phone instructions out loud.
Step 3 — Color-Coded District Filtration
Select any district chip above (North Atlantic, Southeast, Midwest, Continental & Pacific) to isolate regional VISNs. Each facility pin, border, and badge matches its designated VISN color.
Step 4 — Direct Dispatch & TTS Verification
Click the Read Aloud button on any facility card to trigger immediate spoken guidance and visual notification banner confirmation.
