Ride Instructions & Eligibility

Standard Operating Procedure
Eligibility Verification

To qualify for VA Beneficiary Travel transit coordination, you must be 30% or more service-connected, traveling for care of a service-connected condition, or otherwise VA Travel authorized (38 CFR Part 70).

Step 1: Enter Your ZIP Code Enter your five-digit zip code on the previous screen, or use the Voice / GPS buttons.
Step 2: 48-Hour Advance Call Rule Tap the blinking green CALL TRAVEL COORDINATOR button at least 48 hours before your appointment.
Step 3: Have Details Ready Have your Full Name, Last 4 SSN, and Home of Record pickup address ready for verification.
Step 4: Ride Day Text Reply Reply '1' to the automated text when ready for pickup, and reply '2' when your clinic visit is done.

Zero-PII Compliance

Client-Side Architecture

1. Zero Data Logging: No names, Social Security Numbers, or health details are collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Local Browser Execution: 100% of ZIP lookups and dialer routing execute client-side in the veteran's device RAM.

3. Zero Breach Exposure: Fully compliant with VA cybersecurity standards and HIPAA safeguards by never capturing Protected Health Information (PHI).
