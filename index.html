How to Book VA Transportation

Step 1: Enter your 5-digit residential ZIP code or tap VOICE / GPS.

Step 2: Tap the green CALL TRAVEL COORDINATOR button to connect directly with your regional VISN dispatch desk (no call trees).

Step 3: State your last name, last 4 of your SSN, and verify your clinic appointment date/time.

Step 4: Check your mobile SMS messages for ride dispatch vouchers (Uber, Lyft, or local contracted handicap transport).

*Note: VA travel protocol requires scheduling rides at least 48 hours prior to your scheduled clinic appointment.
