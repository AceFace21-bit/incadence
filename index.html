VA Ride Qualification Criteria

38 CFR Part 70 // Beneficiary Travel
Who Automatically Qualifies:

You are eligible for VA Beneficiary Travel transit coordination if you meet any one of the following statutory criteria:

1. 30% or Higher Service Connection

You have a service-connected disability rating of 30% or greater (for care of any medical condition).

2. Care for Service-Connected Conditions

You have a rating under 30%, but you are traveling specifically for the treatment or examination of a service-connected disability.

3. VA Non-Service Connected Pension

You receive a VA disability pension or your annual household income falls below the maximum annual VA pension rate.

4. Scheduled C&P Examinations

You are traveling for an official scheduled Compensation and Pension (C&P) exam ordered by the VA.

5. Special Mode Transportation Authorization

Your doctor or VA clinician has pre-approved specialized transport (wheelchair van, stretcher) due to medical necessity.

Standard 48-Hour Booking Protocol: Always call your travel coordinator at least 48 hours prior to your scheduled clinic appointment.

Ride Instructions & Eligibility

Standard Operating Procedure
Eligibility Verification

To qualify for VA Beneficiary Travel transit coordination, you must be 30% or more service-connected, profile-authorized, or traveling for care of a service-connected condition (38 CFR Part 70).

Zero-PII Compliance

Client-Side Architecture

1. Zero Data Logging: No names, Social Security Numbers, or health details are collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Local Browser Execution: 100% of ZIP lookups and dialer routing execute client-side in the veteran's device RAM.

3. Zero Breach Exposure: Fully compliant with VA cybersecurity standards and HIPAA safeguards by never capturing Protected Health Information (PHI).
